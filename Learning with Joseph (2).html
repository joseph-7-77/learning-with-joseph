About the Founder

Joseph Asiedu Amoah

Visionary Educator, Innovator & Founder of Learning with Joseph

Joseph Asiedu Amoah created Learning with Joseph with one inspiring mission: to make quality, engaging, and modern education accessible to every Grade 6 to Grade 9 student across Ghana and beyond.

"Empowering Young Minds Through Technology & Passion"

Joseph believes every child deserves a patient teacher, crystal-clear explanations, and hands-on digital tools to unlock their greatness.

"A Dedicated Mentor Building Future Leaders"

Through creativity, discipline, and kindness, Joseph Asiedu Amoah transforms complex GES topics in Computing, English, Maths, and Science into joyful learning moments.

"Excellence, Innovation & Heart"

Celebrating hard work every week—because when students are encouraged and guided with love, there is no limit to what they can achieve!
